# Joining in

There's no formal process. Some ways to take part:

- **Try it** on your Bento and tell us what happened, good or bad, in an issue.
- **Suggest features.** Workflow ideas from people who actually play the thing are
  the most valuable part.
- **Fork it** and experiment. If you build something cool, a pull request is
  welcome, and so is just showing it off.

Two things to keep in mind: never modify the bootloader, and don't add
1010music firmware files to the repo.
